Get started

A summer of learning, built for pupils.

The EduPrime Summer Skills Bootcamp introduces pupils to essential digital and technology skills, including Artificial Intelligence, Coding, Product Design (UI/UX), Mobile App Development, Cybersecurity, and Web Design. Through practical, engaging lessons, they learn to think creatively, solve problems, build digital projects, and develop skills that prepare them for the future.

What pupils do

Learn by making things

  • 01

    Explore AI and Coding

    Discover how Artificial Intelligence works, learn coding fundamentals, and create simple digital projects while developing logical thinking and problem-solving skills.

  • 02

    Design and Build Digital Products

    Learn the basics of UI/UX and Web Design, explore how websites and apps are created, and turn creative ideas into simple digital designs.

  • 03

    Create and Stay Safe with Technology

    Explore Mobile App Development and Cybersecurity, learn how digital applications work, and understand how to stay safe and responsible online.

Format

Learn your way, wherever you are

Whether joining virtually or attending in person, pupils enjoy an engaging, hands-on learning experience designed to help them explore technology and build practical skills.

Virtual Learning

  • A laptop or desktop computer with a stable internet connection.
  • Live, instructor-led sessions with interactive lessons, practical activities and guided projects.
  • A quiet learning space where pupils can participate and practise comfortably.

In-Person Learning

  • Physical classes in Jos, Plateau State.
  • Hands-on sessions with guided instruction, practical exercises and collaborative projects.
  • A supportive, supervised learning environment where pupils can ask questions, explore ideas and learn together.

Schedule

Four weeks of learning, creating and discovery

Running across August and September, the bootcamp takes pupils through an exciting journey of technology exploration, hands-on learning and creative project development.

  • Discover the World of Technology Get introduced to Artificial Intelligence, explore how technology shapes everyday life, and learn the fundamentals of coding and computational thinking.
  • Design and Create Explore UI/UX and Web Design, learn how digital products are planned and designed, and turn creative ideas into interactive designs.
  • Build and Stay Secure Discover Mobile App Development, explore how applications work, and learn essential cybersecurity practices for staying safe online.
  • Project Showcase Bring new skills together through practical projects, present completed work, celebrate achievements and receive participation recognition.

Questions from parents

The EduPrime Summer Skills Bootcamp is designed for children aged 8–15 who are curious about technology, creativity and digital innovation. No previous coding or technology experience is required. Beginners are welcome!

The bootcamp runs across August and September, with a structured learning programme spanning four weeks. Registered parents will receive information about the confirmed start date, daily schedule and session times.

The bootcamp is planned to accommodate both virtual and in-person learning. Physical sessions will take place in Jos, Plateau State, while virtual participants can join remotely. Availability of each format will be communicated during registration.

Registration costs ₦30,000 per child. This covers participation in the bootcamp's learning programme, practical sessions and guided project activities. Please contact our team for payment instructions and further details.

Pupils will explore six exciting areas: Artificial Intelligence, Coding, UI/UX Design, Mobile App Development, Cybersecurity and Web Design. They will also develop creativity, critical thinking and problem-solving skills through practical activities.

Not at all! The bootcamp welcomes beginners. Lessons are designed to introduce pupils to technology concepts gradually, with practical guidance and activities suited to their learning levels.

Virtual participants will need a laptop or desktop computer, a reliable internet connection and a quiet learning space. Details about equipment and learning materials for in-person participants will be provided during registration.

Certificates of participation can be presented to pupils who successfully complete the bootcamp, subject to the programme's attendance and completion requirements.

Simply complete the registration form on this page or contact EduPrime Systems through contact@eduprime.ng. Our team will guide you through the registration and payment process.
